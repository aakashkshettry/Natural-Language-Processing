Former BBC Business anchor and global financial commentator Susannah Streeter will headline the second Tech Talk Wales event at the Celtic Manor Resort this month. Streeter, now Chief Investment Strategist at
 Wealth Club, will discuss how technology, investment, and business growth are increasingly intertwined when the event returns on Thursday. The free event will bring together members of Wales' growing technology
 community to discuss emerging trends, investment opportunities, and challenges facing businesses. The discussion will explore emerging trends and market developments alongside the opportunities available to organisations
 seeking to harness new technology for growth.
